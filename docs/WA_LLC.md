# Washington LLC filing costs

Planning note for a Washington limited liability company that stays a
pass-through and has no employees at the start. Fees below are the amounts
published by the Secretary of State and the Department of Revenue as of
September 2026. The online formation cart adds a processing fee on top of the
$180 certificate, so the checkout total is a bit higher than the statutory fee.
This is a budget, not legal or tax advice.

## First year, filed yourself

Skip expedited processing, be your own registered agent, get the EIN from the
IRS, and do not register as an employer.

| Filing | Amount | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate of Formation | $180, plus the online processing fee | Once, Secretary of State |
| Initial report | $0 if included with formation | Due within 120 days. $10 if deferred |
| Business license application | $50 | Once, Department of Revenue. Issues the UBI |
| City endorsement | Set by the city | Added in the same business-license wizard |
| Annual report | $70 | Due the last day of the formation-anniversary month |

That is about $300 in state fees the first year, before the city endorsement
and the online processing fee.

## Secretary of State

File at [ccfs.sos.wa.gov](https://ccfs.sos.wa.gov). The
[LLC filing page](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/start-domestic-wa-limited-liability-company-llc-online)
and the
[fee schedule](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-scheduleexpedited-service)
are the source for these numbers.

| Filing | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of Formation | $180 |
| Online processing | Added at checkout, on top of $180 |
| Expedited processing | $100 extra. Standard processing is enough |
| Initial report, filed with formation | $0 |
| Initial report, filed later | $10 |
| Annual report | $70 |
| Annual report filed after the deadline | $95 ($70 plus a $25 delinquency fee) |
| Name reservation | $30. Optional. A formed LLC does not need this |
| Amendment | $30 |
| Change of registered agent | $0 |
| Dissolution | $0 |
| Reinstatement | $140 plus every missed annual report |
| Certificate of existence | $20 |

The annual report is due by the last day of the month the LLC was formed, and
it can be filed up to 180 days early. Missing it is what takes the LLC out of
active status.

The registered agent must have a physical Washington street address. Serving as
your own agent costs nothing. A commercial agent is commonly $100–$300 a year.
The address on the filing is a public record.

The state does not file or charge for an operating agreement. Write one anyway.
With more than one member it is the record of who owns what.

A name the Secretary of State will accept is only a record-name check. It is
not a trademark clearance.

## Department of Revenue

Apply through the Business Licensing Service. The processing fees are on
[dor.wa.gov](https://dor.wa.gov/open-business/apply-business-license/variable-business-license-processing-fees).

| Filing | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Open the first location of a new business | $50, non-refundable |
| Any later change (trade name, new endorsement, hiring) | $10 |
| Trade name (DBA), if the public name differs from the LLC name | $5 per name |
| Annual renewal handling fee | $5 per location, plus the city's endorsement renewal |
| Late renewal | Half the endorsement fee, capped at $150 per location |

The $50 application is also where a participating city adds its own business
license. The wizard prices that endorsement from the business address. A
home-based software business is often still inside the city requirement.

Tax registration on that same application is $0. An LLC that will sell a
service should register even before revenue, because the UBI is what later
returns are filed under.

## Federal

| Item | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| EIN, Form SS-4, filed at irs.gov | $0 |
| Default pass-through classification | $0. No form is required |
| S-corporation election, Form 2553 | $0 to file. A different tax structure than the default |

A single-member LLC is a disregarded entity. Profit goes on the owner's Form
1040, Schedule C. Two or more members are a partnership and file Form 1065.
Washington has no personal or entity income tax on that profit.

Banks usually want the EIN even when a single-member LLC could use a Social
Security number for income tax. Get it from the IRS. Formation-service "EIN
filing" fees are a resale of a free form.

Federal tax on the profit is income tax plus self-employment tax. Quarterly
estimates use Form 1040-ES. There is no fee to file them. Due dates are
generally April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15.

## No employees

Leave "will hire employees" unchecked on the business license application.

That keeps the LLC out of Labor & Industries workers' compensation and out of
Employment Security unemployment insurance. Both accounts are free to open.
Once the unemployment account exists, a quarterly wage report is due even when
payroll is zero.

Owner draws are not wages. The default pass-through does not put members on
payroll. Hiring the first employee later is a $10 update to the existing
business license, and that update is what opens workers' compensation and
unemployment.

Paid Family and Medical Leave and WA Cares follow the same employment
registration. They are not part of formation.

## Tax that is not a filing fee

Business-and-occupation tax is on gross receipts, not profit. A software or
hosted-service business is often classified as service and other activities.
The small-business B&O credit often reduces the tax to $0 at modest revenue.
The return is still filed once the business is registered.

For businesses that report at least half their income as service and other
activities, the credit applies while total B&O liability stays under these
amounts (periods beginning on or after January 1, 2023):

| Filing frequency | Credit available while B&O liability is under |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | $320 |
| Quarterly | $960 |
| Annual | $3,840 |

The credit is a sliding scale, not a flat exemption. It phases out as the tax
bill grows. A larger credit and a $250,000 annual filing threshold are
scheduled for January 1, 2029, not for the current year. Source:
[DOR credits](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/tax-incentives/credits).

Retail sales tax is separate from the $50 license fee. Software and some online
services sold to Washington customers can be taxable, at the state rate plus
the local rate for the buyer's location.

## What not to buy

A formation package that quotes a price on top of these filings is reselling
the same Secretary of State certificate, the same Department of Revenue
application, and the same free EIN. The state and IRS portals are the ones that
set the price.
