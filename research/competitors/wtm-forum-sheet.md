## Shift over time

| Era | Loudest names in this dataset |
| --- | --- |
| 2008–2012 | **Homeschool Tracker**, Skedtrack, Scholaric, Helper, Charlotte Mason Organizer, Well Planned Day |
| 2013–2016 | **Scholaric** and **Homeschool Planet** rise; Tracker quieter |
| 2017–2020 | **Homeschool Planet** leads; Google Classroom appears; dataset ends 2020 |

## How this complements our other research

- Confirms long-running names we already track: **Homeschool Planet**, **Scholaric**, **Homeschool Tracker**.
- Surfaces older / adjacent tools worth a short glossary pass (not all still active): **Skedtrack**, **Homeschool Helper**, **Well Planned Day Online**, **Charlotte Mason Organizer**, **Lessontrek**, **Homeschool Minder**.
- Shows the community conversation moved from “recordkeeping / scheduling utilities” toward **Planet-style all-in-one planners**, with Classroom as a generic school tool crossover late in the series.
- Does **not** cover Syllabird, Homeschool Panda, or the OSS peers we added separately—those are newer or outside this forum crawl.

## Caveats

- WTM forum ≠ whole market; Ambleside/CM-heavy threads bias the early years.
- A “1” means mentioned in that thread, not endorsed.
- Sheet stops at 2020; treat as historical baseline, then layer 2024–2026 marketing/OSS research on top.
